# Nova UI/UX Pro Rules

1. Do not redesign a locked product without explicit approval.
2. Do not introduce visual effects because they are fashionable; tie them to PRD/TRD goals.
3. Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for glass, shaders, 3D or motion.
4. Do not use color as the only state indicator.
5. Do not hide errors behind generic success states.
6. Do not ship a blank state when data is loading, empty, unavailable or failed.
7. Do not assume desktop composition can be cropped into a good mobile experience.
8. Do not assume a WebGL effect works on every device; provide a fallback.
9. Do not run expensive effects on every component when one composited layer is sufficient.
10. Do not add a library until its stack/version compatibility and bundle/performance impact are understood.
11. Do not expose secrets, tokens, stack traces or private identifiers in client UI.
12. Do not claim testing or verification that was not performed.
13. Preserve third-party licenses and attribution when code or assets are directly reused.
14. Keep the approved Master Design System as the source of truth across subsequent screens.
15. Record significant visual-direction decisions so later implementation does not drift.
